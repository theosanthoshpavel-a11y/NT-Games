Unit 1 · A time-travel expedition through four empires

Into the Silent Years

Lesson 1 Historical Context · Lesson 2 Religious Context · Lesson 3 Cultural Contexts

You have just finished reading Malachi and turned the page to Matthew. Suddenly you meet synagogues, Pharisees, Herod, Roman soldiers, Hanukkah and the Septuagint, and Matthew never stops to explain them. Your mission: travel back through the “silent years” and recover the world his first readers already knew. Each challenge unlocks a stage of the journey from the Persian return to a Roman cross.
