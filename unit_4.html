Unit 4 · Break the seals on letters written to real communities

Letters to the Churches

Lesson 10 Pauline Epistles · Lesson 11 General Epistles · Lesson 12 Revelation

A courier arrives with a satchel of sealed letters. Each was written to a real church facing real problems: divisions, false teaching, suffering, favouritism and the pressure to compromise. Break each seal by discovering who wrote, to whom, and why, and finish by unveiling a vision of the Lamb who stands at the centre of history.
