Signs of the Kingdom

Lesson 13 The Kingdom, Christ, and Salvation · Lesson 14 The Church, Mission, and New Creation

After a flood, a village needs more than a promise of escape. It needs clean water, restored homes, healing and justice. Your final mission is to trace the threads that hold the New Testament together: God’s reign announced in Jesus, the Messiah who serves and suffers, a salvation bigger than escape, and a Spirit-filled people sent to bear witness.
