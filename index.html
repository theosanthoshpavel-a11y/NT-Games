Introduction to the New Testament: Learning Games

Five interactive games, one for each unit of the course. Explore the world behind the text, the making of the canon, the Gospels and Acts, the letters and Revelation, and the theology that holds the New Testament together.

How to play

  • Choose a unit below. Each game has 12 or 13 challenges and works on its own, so you can play the units in any order.
  • Activities include detective clues, sequencing, matching, sorting, story maps, branching scenarios, word puzzles and short reflections.
  • You get instant feedback and an explanation from the course guide after every challenge. Your score, progress and final rank are shown as you play.
  • Answer choices are shuffled every time, so replaying is real practice, not memorising positions.
