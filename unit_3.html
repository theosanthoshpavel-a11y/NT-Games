Unit 3 · Follow the good news from Galilee to Rome

Witnesses on the Road

Lesson 7 Synoptic Gospels · Lesson 8 Gospel of John · Lesson 9 Acts

A messenger runs into the city shouting “Good news!” But what kind of news is it, and who is the king it announces? Travel with four Gospel writers and one historian as they tell the story of Jesus and the mission he continues through the Spirit. Gather clues, follow the road from Galilee to Jerusalem, and carry the witness “to the ends of the earth.”
